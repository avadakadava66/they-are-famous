July 2024

A student-led movement for quota reform grew into a nationwide uprising. The UN human rights office later estimated that up to 1,400 people were killed between July and August 2024. Each name is a person, not a number.

1952 and 1971

The Bangla Language Movement of 1952 grew into the Liberation War of 1971. It began on the night of 25 March and ended in victory on 16 December. Bir Sreshtho, the highest gallantry award, honours seven heroes.
